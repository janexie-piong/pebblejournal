privacy

pebble works without an account, and your journal stays on your device. if you choose to create an account, we store only what's needed to sync your journal: your email, your name, and your entries (including the city you wrote from). your data is stored securely with our service provider, supabase. we don't use ads, analytics, or trackers, and we never sell or share your data. to delete your account and data, email support@pebblejournal.app.
